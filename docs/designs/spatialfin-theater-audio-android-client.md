*   **Author:** Ignacio Martinez <ignacio@martinez.fyi>
*   **Status:** Proposed

## Overview

This document defines **Spec 1**, the Android client design connecting **SpatialFin** on **Samsung Galaxy XR** (Full Space) and **Beam phones** (SpatialFin's phone and USB-C DisplayPort Alt Mode `Presentation` flavor) to the standalone Rust **Theater-Audio Service** (`spatialfin-avr`, [Spec 2](./spatialfin-avr-rust-service-and-hardware.md)) over the versioned **Spec 0** wire contract ([theater-audio-protocol-v1.md](./theater-audio-protocol-v1.md); see Spec 0's *Glossary & External Dependencies* for shared wireless theater terms).

SpatialFin remains the sole Jellyfin session owner (authentication, watch-progress reporting, track selection, and `DeviceProfile` negotiation) and video renderer. On Android, SpatialFin uses a two-process architecture: the **main process** hosts cast discovery, pairing, encrypted token storage, Beam video playback, and XR calibration, whereas `:xrplayer` is an isolated OS process dedicated to Galaxy XR `SurfaceEntity` video rendering. Selecting a `spatialfin-avr` room from SpatialFin's cast picker disables local audio, launches lossless direct-stream audio via an authenticated Spec 0 WebSocket Remote Procedure Call (RPC) engine (`SendspinAvr`), and keeps local video locked at `1.0000×` native cadence. Spec 1 uses the normative wire identifier `CastProtocol.SendspinAvr` (`"SendspinAvr:<id>"` per Spec 0), which is distinct from SpatialFin's untouched `:sendspin` music module.

Beyond core Split Audio/Video (`Split A/V`) playback, Spec 1 adds **Tier 2** USB-C On-The-Go (`OTG`) calibrated microphone (`UMIK-1`) capture on Beam phones (alongside **Tier 1** Room EQ Wizard `REW` / `Cavern QuickEQ` CamillaDSP filter bundle uploads over HTTP, upgrading from server **Tier 0** speaker-geometry defaults), and single-anchor Galaxy XR 6-Degrees-of-Freedom (`6DoF`) speaker pinning with a reversible Left-Center-Right (`LCR`) virtual screen lock.

## The Problem

Codebase inspection of the SpatialFin repository surfaced six blockers preventing integration with `spatialfin-avr`:

1.  **Split A/V is hard-wired to FCast, and module boundaries constrain placement:** `SplitAvController` injects `FCastCastingController` directly; `CastSessionManager`, `SplitAvPolicy`, and `FCastSessionPickerSheet` gate on `CastProtocol.FCast` (SpatialFin's existing LAN casting protocol with a 32 KiB message ceiling); `:sendspin` already depends one-way on `:fcast` (blocking `:fcast -> :sendspin`); `:fcast:session-ui` cannot import `:player:xr` due to `:player:beam`'s single dex-merge guardrail; and adding `CastProtocol.SendspinAvr` triggers an exhaustive `when` compile failure in `FCastSessionUi.protocolMonogram()`.
2.  **Non-FLAC lossless/surround codecs are penalized, `LOCAL` sources win by `+500`, and URL tokens leak:** `CastSessionManager.chooseSplitAvMediaSource()` applies a `-80` penalty outside `{aac, mp4a-latm, opus, vorbis, flac, mp3}` and a `+500` bonus to `SpatialFinSourceType.LOCAL`, while `SplitAvAudioRoutePolicy.canDirect()` in `castSpatialItemSplitAv()` forces Jellyfin transcodes unless the receiver advertises matching tokens. `withJellyfinAuth()` appends `?api_key=` to stream URLs, and `CastSessionManager.castViaAdapter()` unconditionally logs `url.take(200)` at `Timber.e` level.
3.  **Video speed-trimming, hardcoded AVR offsets, and missing `T_play` / theater IPC:** `SplitAvController` steers `ExoPlayer.setPlaybackSpeed()` via `NudgeController` (perturbing 24p/90 Hz XR cadence and conflicting with `SurfaceFrameRateController`) and adds `+35–60 ms` via `AudioLatencyProfile.extraLatencyMs`. `SplitAvBridgeIpc` carries millisecond timestamps only, lacks two-step keyframe-hold `T_play` (`MSG_PREPARE_HOLD` / `MSG_FIRST_FRAME_READY` / `MSG_START_AT_BOOTTIME_US`), buffering-stall, track-switch, and episode-change IPC, and lacks a cross-process state/control envelope for telemetry, geometry, volume, `EQ Bypass`, and XR preferences; meanwhile `SplitAvVideoTiming.sampledMonotonicMs` is still required locally by legacy FCast.
4.  **Abrupt `:xrplayer` teardown races, `ContentKeyManager` mismatches, and Beam playback gaps:** When finishing, `XrPlayerActivity.onPause()` exits via `Process.killProcess()` before `onDestroy()`, `SplitAvBridgeService` lacks `IBinder.DeathRecipient`, and `SplitAvController` idles in `AwaitingMaster` without cold-start heartbeats or immediate `USER_EXIT` teardown. `ContentKeyManager` wraps a 32-byte offline-download DEK tied to App Lock resets rather than general room-token storage. On Beam, `BeamPlayerActivity`'s first-frame audio gate and 15 s anti-hang backstop conflict with `T_play`, `onResume()`/`onTracksChanged` re-enable `C.TRACK_TYPE_AUDIO`, `onNewIntent()` leaks listeners, and `BeamItemDetailScreen.onChapterClick` bypasses `launchServerItem`.
5.  **Existing chirp calibration has a clock-base bug and lacks UMIK-1 streaming capture:** `CalibrationCapture` mixes `AudioTimestamp.TIMEBASE_MONOTONIC` (`CLOCK_MONOTONIC`) with `SystemClock.elapsedRealtime()` (`CLOCK_BOOTTIME`), causing multi-hour drift after suspend; `CalibrationCapture` loops indefinitely on negative USB `AudioRecord.read()` errors; and `WavWriter` buffers entire PCM payloads in RAM instead of streaming 24-bit WAV files to disk for pure-HTTP `UMIK-1` uploads.
6.  **Ephemeral XR coordinates, destructive `WallAnchor` collision, and `:xrplayer`'s 15-key preference allowlist:** Android XR `ActivitySpace` resets across kills; manual recenter in `SpatialPlayerScreen` calls `WallAnchor.unpersist()` on `pref_xr_player_wall_anchor_uuid` (overwriting/erasing any shared room anchor across rooms); and `AppPreferences.XR_PLAYER_PREF_KEYS` (enforced by `ArchitectureGuardsTest`) throws on any `:xrplayer` write outside those 15 keys.

## Goals

*   **Phase 1 — Engine-Separated Split A/V, Pairing & Lossless Playback (XR + Beam):** Replace FCast coupling with a narrow `SplitAvSessionEngine` seam (`FCastSplitAvEngine` + `SendspinAvrSplitAvEngine`) inside `SplitAvController`, add `AudioCodecTokens` in `:fcast` (`19` tokens, delegated by `:player:core` `ReceiverAudioCodecs`), discover `_spatialfin-avr._tcp` via internal `JmdnsHelper`, pair via PIN/QR/`host:port` with a dedicated non-auth-bound `AndroidKeyStore` AES-256-GCM `TheaterAvrTokenStore`, select online `REMOTE` Jellyfin direct streams matching `MediaStreamLanguage`, enforce Spec 0 §3 URL/token sanitization, run native `1.0000×` keyframe-first `T_play` + `2 Hz` beacons + `2 s` heartbeats + full Spec 0 error/event handling + synchronous `USER_EXIT` / `IBinder.DeathRecipient` teardown + Beam parity, and expose shared XR/Beam HUD controls, structured `Timber` observability, `SplitAvDebugBridge`, and `schemas/theater-audio/v1/` contract tests.
*   **Phase 2 — Pure-HTTP Room Calibration & Tier 2 Beam OTG UMIK-1 Capture:** Fix `CalibrationCapture` `TIMEBASE_BOOTTIME`, introduce `:core` `VoiceCaptureSuspensionRegistry` to suspend `SpatialVoiceService`/`BeamVoiceService`/`HomeVoiceController` during capture, stream 24-bit/48 kHz UMIK-1 WAV files to disk (`Pre-open AudioRecord`, relative server duration, live peak digital full-scale `dBFS` headroom meter, disconnect abort), and deliver pure-HTTP `TheaterCalibrationScreen` for speaker mapping, stepped Sound Pressure Level (`SPL`) checks, Tier 2 sweep + Storage Access Framework (`SAF`) `.txt` + `metadata_json` multipart upload, single-channel re-measure, and Tier 1 `/bundles` upload/apply/revert.
*   **Phase 3 — Galaxy XR Single-Anchor 6DoF Pinning, Dark-Room Center Snap & Telemetry HUD:** Host `XrRoomCalibrationOverlay` in main-process `UnifiedMainActivity` XR Full Space `Session` using `internal object WallAnchor` to pin speaker coordinates into the canonical Room Coordinate Frame + `T_anchor_to_mlp`, store per-`server_id` `{ anchorUuid, anchorToMlpTransform }` in main-process `RememberedReceiversStore` (zero `:xrplayer` allowlist expansion and zero `WallAnchor.unpersist()` collision), spawn frame-0 gravity screen + one-shot `<= 2_500 ms` startup optical restore, add 0-lux 2-button `Center Snap` + reversible `Screen Locked to Room LCR ↔ Free Screen Placement` HUD toggle, and render live `Theater Audio` metrics in `PlaybackStatsOverlay`.

## Non-Goals

*   Upgrading `:sendspin` (`sendspin-jvm:0.2.3`) for Late-Night Binaural Mode in v1 (deferred; v1 quiet listening uses 1-tap `Headset / Room Audio` fold-back).
*   Supporting XR Home Space (`MultitaskPlayerActivity`), Android TV (`TvPlayerActivity`), or `spatialfin-webxr` in v1.
*   Supporting non-Jellyfin `PlayRequest` sources (local files, SMB/NFS, plugins, or offline-only downloads) in v1.
*   Running Room EQ filter DSP solvers on Android (Android records and uploads WAV + `.txt`; solving runs in `spatialfin-avr`, Spec 2).

## Open Questions

*   **Galaxy XR USB-C PD-Hub OTG Check:** Validate on-device whether Galaxy XR (`SM-I610`) exposes `UMIK-1` via `AudioManager.GET_DEVICES_INPUTS` over a powered USB-C PD pass-through hub during Phase 2 calibration bring-up; Beam phone `OTG` and Spec 2's laptop `avr-capture-cli` provide Tier 2 capture paths regardless of Galaxy XR OTG support.
*   **HLS Transcode Resume Priming Offset (Spec 0 Phase 0 Check 2):** Validate residual PTS offset across Jellyfin HLS fMP4/TS transcodes relative to direct containers so `SplitAvStreamUrlPolicy` local HLS PTS normalization stays accurate across post-resume seeks.

## Critical User Journeys (CUJs)

### CUJ 1: Pair, Pick & Play Lossless 7.1.4 on Galaxy XR or Beam Phone

*   **Given:** `spatialfin-avr` advertises `_spatialfin-avr._tcp` on the LAN, or the user pairs via QR URI (`spatialfin-avr://pair?...`) or manual `host:port` (with TLS option) on a segmented VLAN.
*   **When:** User pairs via 6-digit PIN or QR code (storing the minted Bearer token encrypted in `AndroidKeyStore`), selects the room, and starts a Jellyfin TrueHD Atmos movie (including via a Beam chapter tap).
*   **Then:** SpatialFin selects the online remote direct-stream lossless source matching the user's pre-playback chip selection, disables local audio (`StandaloneMediaClock`), holds playback paused on the target Instantaneous Decoder Refresh (`IDR`) keyframe, executes `playback.ready(actual_pts_us) -> rpc.ok(t_play_avr_us)`, releases the video frame at `t_play_client_us = t_play_avr_us - theta_us - display_offset_us` (`CLOCK_BOOTTIME`), locks `1.0000×` cadence, and exposes hardware/HUD master volume, `active-codec badge`, `upmix_mode`, `Headset / Room Audio` fold-back, and delay-invariant 1-tap `EQ Bypass` (`room.set_eq_bypass`).

### CUJ 2: Direct-Play Track Switch, Pause, Abrupt Exit & App Lock Cleanup

*   **Given:** Active `SendspinAvr` session on Galaxy XR (`:xrplayer`) or Beam.
*   **When:** User switches audio tracks, seeks, pauses, triggers App Lock, taps `Headset / Room Audio`, or exits via Back (`Process.killProcess()` when finishing).
*   **Then:** Audio track switching lists tracks from Jellyfin metadata (`uiState.currentMediaStreams`) and sends `playback.load` (incrementing `barrier_seq`) to re-align via `playback.ready -> rpc.ok`; Pause flushes room audio in `< 15 ms` (resuming via `playback.ready`); App Lock, user switch, and `Headset / Room Audio` fold-back send `session.stop` within `250 ms` (restoring local audio on fold-back); intentional Back-exit sends synchronous `MSG_UNREGISTER(USER_EXIT)` (`0 ms` grace → `< 250 ms` stop), while unexpected `:xrplayer` death fires `IBinder.DeathRecipient`, pauses room audio (`< 15 ms`), and stops the session after `1.5 s`.

### CUJ 3: Pure-HTTP Tier 1 Bundle Upload & Tier 2 UMIK-1 Calibration on Beam Phone

*   **Given:** Beam phone on LAN (glasses unplugged or powered USB-C PD + DisplayPort Alt Mode hub) with calibrated `UMIK-1` mic attached and its per-serial `.txt` compensation file selected once via Android's `SAF` picker (`0°` / `90°` toggle persisted via `takePersistableUriPermission`).
*   **When:** User either uploads a Tier 1 `REW` / `Cavern QuickEQ` ZIP (`POST /api/v1/calibration/bundles`) or runs a guided Tier 2 sweep: chimes speakers to confirm `connected_endpoints` mapping, checks headroom via manual stepped chimes (`POST /api/v1/calibration/chime` with live peak `dBFS` meter and preset gain chips `<= -12.0 dBFS`), and starts a bracketed sweep (`POST /api/v1/calibration/sweeps`).
*   **Then:** For Tier 2, SpatialFin acquires `VoiceCaptureSuspensionRegistry`, pre-opens `AudioRecord` (`setPreferredDevice`, `UNPROCESSED` probe + fallback warning, `ENCODING_PCM_FLOAT` packed to 24-bit/48 kHz streaming WAV on disk across relative duration `(tend_ref_avr_us - t0_ref_avr_us) + lead + 1.5 s`), uploads WAV + `.txt` + `metadata_json` via HTTP multipart to `POST /api/v1/calibration/sweeps/{sweep_id}/upload`, renders per-channel Signal-to-Noise Ratio (`SNR`), clipping, and `calibration_id` single-channel re-measure cards, activates the staged Room EQ via `POST /api/v1/calibration/apply` (or rolls back via `/revert`), and leaves `EQ Bypass` (`0 µs` lip-sync shift) available in the playback HUD for A/B listening.

### CUJ 4: Galaxy XR Single-Anchor 6DoF Pinning & Dark-Room Acoustic Center-Snap

*   **Given:** Galaxy XR in passthrough mode with `SCENE_UNDERSTANDING_COARSE` granted, tested in both lit rooms and `0 lux` theaters.
*   **When:** In main-process `XrRoomCalibrationOverlay`, user gazes at seated Main Listening Position (`MLP`), anchors the front wall once (`WallAnchor`), taps speaker channels to play localization chimes and pin 3D positions into the Room Coordinate Frame (`PUT /api/v1/room/profile`), and later launches a movie in lit or `0 lux` conditions.
*   **Then:** XR spawns the flat virtual cinema screen at frame 0 from gravity + saved `lcr_screen_plane` dimensions/depth while running a one-shot startup optical restore (`<= 2_500 ms`) against the room's persisted `{ anchorUuid, anchorToMlpTransform }`. When optical tracking succeeds, the screen locks centered between Left and Right physical speakers (`Screen Locked to Room LCR`, reversible to `Free Screen Placement`). When `0 lux` optical restore times out, SpatialFin displays a non-blocking banner with `Play Center Chime` (`200 ms` Front Center `FC` cue per tap) and `Lock Here` to align the Screen+LCR frame along the user's facing ray without disturbing `pref_xr_player_wall_anchor_uuid`.

## Technical Plan (Architecture)

### 1. Module Dependency Boundaries & Process Model

```text
:app:unified [main process] (FCastModule DI, PlaybackLauncher, NavigationRoot, SplitAvDebugBridge, XrRoomCalibrationOverlay)
   |--> :fcast:session-ui (SplitAvSessionEngine, SplitAvController, CastSessionManager, RememberedReceiversStore, SplitAvBridgeService, TheaterCalibration*)
   |       |--> :fcast (AudioCodecTokens, CastProtocol, CastCapability, CastReceiver, JmdnsHelper, SendspinAvr*)
   |       |--> :settings (TheaterAvrTokenStore, AppPreferences, AppDataWiper)
   |       |--> :core (VoiceCaptureSuspensionRegistry, SessionModule PreWipeHook)
   |       |--> :data (MediaStreamLanguage, newBuilderWithSsrfGuard)
   |       +--> :player:core (SplitAvVideoMaster, SplitAvBridgeIpc, SplitAvTheaterBridge, TheaterSessionSnapshot, RoomGeometryContract, TheaterAudioTelemetry, ReceiverAudioCodecs)
   |--> :shell:beam [main process] (BeamNavigationRoot, BeamItemDetailScreen, BeamJellyfinHelpers)
   +--> :player:beam [main process] --api--> :player:xr [:xrplayer process] (XrPlayerActivity, SpatialPlayerScreen, PlayerSecondaryControls, PlayerControlPanel, WallAnchor, PlaybackStatsOverlay)
                                               +--> :player:local (PlayerViewModel, PlayerTrackSelector, PlaybackStatsCollector)
                                               +--> :player:core
(:sendspin keeps one-way dep :sendspin -> :fcast; untouched in v1)
```

*   **No Gradle cycles:** Pure-Kotlin `AudioCodecTokens` lives in `:fcast`, while `ReceiverAudioCodecs` (`:player:core`) delegates token matching to `:fcast` (`player/core` depends on `:fcast`, never the reverse).
*   **Zero `:fcast:session-ui -> :player:xr` edge:** `:player:beam`'s single dex-merge configuration merges compiled library bytecode and resources from `:player:xr` only along `:app:unified -> :player:beam --api--> :player:xr`; `:fcast:session-ui` never imports `:player:xr`. Spatial geometry, telemetry, and HUD commands cross via `:player:core` (`SplitAvTheaterBridge` + `SplitAvBridgeIpc`), mic suspension crosses via `:core` (`VoiceCaptureSuspensionRegistry`), and `XrRoomCalibrationOverlay` lives in `:app:unified` (main process, using `WallAnchor` transitively via `:player:beam`).
*   **`:xrplayer` 15-key preference allowlist untouched:** `AppPreferences.XR_PLAYER_PREF_KEYS` (checked statically and at runtime by `ArchitectureGuardsTest`) remains at 15 keys. AVR tokens and `{ anchorUuid, anchorToMlpTransform }` live in main-process stores, and `pref_xr_theater_lcr_screen_lock` (`Boolean`, default `true`) mutates only in the main process via `MSG_THEATER_EVENT`.

### 2. Transport, Engine Seam & Session Orchestration

#### Wire Models, Codec Tokens, Discovery & HTTP (`:fcast`)

*   Create `AudioCodecTokens.kt` inside `:fcast` holding the exact `19`-token union referenced by Spec 0 and `TheaterAudioSchemaContractTest`:
    *   `UNIVERSAL_SOFTWARE` (`13`): `aac`, `mp3`, `opus`, `vorbis`, `flac`, `alac`, `pcm`, `pcm_s16le`, `pcm_s24le`, `pcm_s32le`, `pcm_f32le`, `pcm_mulaw`, `pcm_alaw`.
    *   `ALL_PASSTHROUGH_TOKENS` (`6`): `ac3`, `eac3`, `eac3-joc`, `truehd`, `dts`, `dts-hd`.
    *   `normalize()`, `canRenderDirect()`, and `preferredTranscodeCodecs()` delegated by `:player:core` `ReceiverAudioCodecs`.
*   Add `CastProtocol.SendspinAvr("Theater Audio")`, `CastCapability.TheaterAudioV1`, and `val useTls: Boolean = false` on `CastReceiver` (`CastCapability.SplitAv` KDoc updated for FCast + `SendspinAvr`; note `CastProtocol.kt` KDoc alignment). Leave `CastAdapterFactory` and `CastDeps` free of `SendspinAvr` (full-A/V `ProtocolAdapter` casting does not apply to audio-only Split A/V).
*   Add `SendspinAvrProtocolModels.kt` (`v=1`, `ignoreUnknownKeys = true`) and shared `SendspinAvrRequestValidator` (invoked identically by runtime code and `TheaterAudioSchemaContractTest` to enforce URL sanitization, `\r`/`\n`/`\0` rejection, Jellyfin-only source checks, and `min_protocol_version`).
*   Extract internal `JmdnsHelper.kt` in `:fcast` reusing `FCastDiscovery`'s `WifiManager.MulticastLock` and bindable Wi-Fi IPv4 interface logic, plus `SendspinAvrDiscoveryBrowser` browsing `_spatialfin-avr._tcp.local.` and validating mandatory TXT records (`v == "1"`, `id` matching `^avr-[0-9a-f-]{8,64}$`, `auth == "bearer"`) → `CastReceiver(id = "SendspinAvr:${txt["id"]}", ...)`.
*   `SendspinAvrTransport` speaks Spec 0 `/ws/v1/control` (`Authorization: Bearer`, uniform `rpc.ok`/`rpc.error` replies, `hello` capability manifest, sliding-window min-RTT `clock.ping` filter: `8` initial samples before playback, `16`-sample / `30 s` bounded window, `clock_uncertainty_us = (rtt_min_spread_us / 2) + sample_age_drift_us` targeting `< 2_000 µs` under `<= 4 ms` RTT spread + `1 Hz` maintenance pings, and `5 s` `playback.ready -> rpc.ok` barrier). `connect`, `startSession`, `signalReady`, `pause`, `stall`, `loadMedia` (`playback.load` supporting `stream_layout`: `multi_stream_container` vs `single_audio_stream` replacement URL), `setVolume`, `setEqBypass`, `setUpmixMode`, `centerSnap`, `lipSyncChirp` (optional WS diagnostic), `stopSession`, and `disconnect` are suspend RPCs; `sendVideoTiming` and `sendHeartbeat` are one-way WebSocket notifications (no `id`).
*   `SendspinAvrHttpClient` uses an SSRF-guarded `OkHttpClient` injected from `:app:unified` `FCastModule` (`okHttpClient.newBuilderWithSsrfGuard(UrlTargetPolicy.LAN_ONLY, allowCrossOriginRedirects = false)` + `followRedirects(false)` / `followSslRedirects(false)` to prevent `Authorization` header leaks on redirects), implementing `/api/v1/pair/claim`, `/api/v1/pair/pin`, `/api/v1/clients/{client_id}`, `/api/v1/info`, `/api/v1/status` (`connected_endpoints`), `/api/v1/room/profile` (`ETag`/`If-Match`), and calibration routes (`/api/v1/calibration/{chime,center-snap,sweeps,sweeps/{sweep_id},sweeps/{sweep_id}/upload,bundles,apply,revert}`).

#### Token Store, Pairing UI, Receiver Store & Lossless Source Selection

*   `TheaterAvrTokenStore` (`:settings`) encrypts per-AVR tokens (`{ server_id -> { avr_token, host, port, use_tls, paired_at_ms } }`) and persistent `client_id` (`client-{uuid}`) under a dedicated non-auth-bound `AndroidKeyStore` AES-256-GCM alias (`spatialfin_theater_avr_tokens_v1`), serialized via `org.json.JSONObject` + Base64 12-byte IV/ciphertext with `server_id` AAD (decoupled from `ContentKeyManager`'s download DEK). On `AppDataWiper` (`PreWipeHook` in `core/.../SessionModule.kt` + `AppDataWiper.kt`) or unpair (`DELETE /api/v1/clients/{client_id}`), tokens and Keystore alias are deleted; `AEADBadTagException` clears the unreadable record and triggers re-pair.
*   `FCastSessionPickerSheet` and `TheaterPairingDialog` (`:fcast:session-ui`) own pairing UX: 6-digit PIN entry, QR scanner trigger (delegated to `NavigationRoot` / `BeamNavigationRoot` which own CameraX/MLKit, parsing `spatialfin-avr://pair?host=...&port=...&id=...&tls=0|1`), manual `host:port` + TLS toggle, secondary-device PIN arming (`POST /api/v1/pair/pin`), client revocation (`DELETE /api/v1/clients/{client_id}`), and `auth_invalid` (`401`) re-pair prompts.
*   `RememberedReceiversStore` adds nullable additive JSON fields (`id`, `protocol`, `useTls`, `cachedSupportedAudioCodecs`, `anchorUuid`, `anchorToMlpTransform`, plus existing manual ±ms display-trim field; `ignoreUnknownKeys = true`), backfills legacy FCast entries as `id = "FCast:$host:$port"`, exempts entries where `protocol == SendspinAvr && tokenStore.hasToken(entry.id)` from `pruneStale()`, caches `GET /api/v1/info` `supported_audio_codecs`, and updates `(host, port)` in place when mDNS resolves an existing `SendspinAvr:<id>` at a new IP.
*   `CastSessionManager` migrates `castSpatialItemSplitAv` / `isSplitAvTransportActive()` from `_pickedReceiver` to `_pickedTarget`, wires `stopCast()` to call `splitAvController.stop()`, updates all `when (CastProtocol)` branches (`shouldShowProtocol(SendspinAvr) = true`), gates `RECORD_AUDIO` and `calibrationOrchestrator.calibrate()` on `CastProtocol.FCast` alone, and restricts `SendspinAvr` Split A/V to `SpatialFinMovie`/`SpatialFinEpisode` while online (`ActiveSessionObserver`) with `SpatialFinSourceType.REMOTE` sources only (removing `+500 LOCAL` bias). `chooseSplitAvMediaSource()` honors `PlayRequest` explicit `mediaSourceIndex`/`audioStreamIndex` via a unified `SplitAvLaunchPlan` passed identically to the local player Intent, reuses `MediaStreamLanguage.matchesLanguage()`, ranks direct-stream lossless codecs matching cached `supported_audio_codecs`, actively strips and validates `stream_url` (rejecting URL userinfo and case-insensitive `api_key=` / `token=` query parameters with `?` or `&`, including `transcodingUrl`), enforces `\r`, `\n`, `\0` rejection on `jellyfin_token`, scrubs logged URLs unconditionally in `castViaAdapter()`, and extends `observeLockAndSessionTeardown()` to clear in-memory token references and stop AVR sessions within `250 ms` on `app_lock` or `user_switch`.

#### Split A/V Engine Seam, Inverted Sync & Spec 0 Error/Event Matrix

*   Introduce `SplitAvSessionEngine` in `:fcast:session-ui` with two concrete engines selected by `CastProtocol` inside a thin `SplitAvController` dispatcher (`SplitAvEngineFactory`): `FCastSplitAvEngine` (preserving existing FCast drift control untouched) and `SendspinAvrSplitAvEngine` (driving `SendspinAvrTransport` directly with lock-free active-job cancellation + `200 ms` bounded `session.stop` RPC in parallel with `stopCast()`).
*   `SendspinAvrSplitAvEngine` locks `ExoPlayer` speed at `1.0000×`, skips `AudioLatencyProfile.extraLatencyMs`, initializes `barrier_seq = 1` on `session.start`, starts `2_000 ms` one-way `session.heartbeat(state="starting")` **upon `session.start` `rpc.ok`** (independent of `:xrplayer` `activeMaster` binding so `6_000 ms` `heartbeat_ttl_ms` cannot starve during cold start), and continues `2_000 ms` heartbeats across `starting`, `awaiting_go`, `paused`, and `stalled`.
*   On seek (`MSG_USER_SEEK`), `SendspinAvrSplitAvEngine` sends `playback.pause(flush = true)` without delay, suppresses `userSeeks` re-emission, and debounces `150 ms` before starting a two-step `T_play` barrier: `master.prepareAndHoldAt(targetPtsMs)` → `firstFrameReadyPtsUs` → `transport.signalReady(barrierSeq, actualPtsUs)` → compute `t_play_client_us = go.tPlayAvrUs - thetaUs - clientDisplayOffsetUs` (`clientDisplayOffsetUs = 0 µs` default plus optional manual per-receiver ms trim; `thetaUs = t_avr - t_client`) → `master.startAtBoottimeUs(tPlayClientUs)` (retrying `barrierSeq + 1` if `t_play_client_us - now_client_us < 1_000 µs`). `PlayerSplitAvAdapter` records `t_play_launch_error_us = actual_release_boottime_us - t_play_client_us` for observability and emits `2 Hz` 4-field `playback.video_timing` (`{ barrier_seq, media_pts_us, visible_avr_us, clock_uncertainty_us }`, where `visible_avr_us = release_boottime_us + display_offset_us + theta_us`).

| Condition / Spec 0 Signal | Client Disposition |
| --- | --- |
| `session_busy` (`409` / `rpc.error`) | Prompt user (`Room In Use — Take Over?`); retry `session.start(force_takeover = true)` only on explicit confirm. |
| `session.released` (`pause_timeout`, `preempted`, `server_shutdown`, `fatal_decode_error`) | Pause local video, update HUD status, show banner with `Reconnect` or `Play on Headset` (`foldBackToLocal()`). |
| `auth_invalid` (`401`) / `jellyfin_auth_failed` (`502`) | `401`: delete stale room token from `TheaterAvrTokenStore` and open `TheaterPairingDialog`; `502`: fold back + surface Jellyfin auth error. |
| `codec_unsupported` (`422`) / `speaker_offline_critical` (`503`) | Stop AVR, execute `foldBackToLocal()`, and show toast naming missing codec or offline `L/C/R` speaker. |
| `5 s` `playback.ready` timeout / `seek_timeout` / `> 3` missed-lead retries | Abort barrier, execute `foldBackToLocal()`, log structured `Timber` warning, and display sync failure banner. |
| `4th` `playback.resync_required` in `30 s` / `clock_uncertainty_us >= 2_000 µs` | `4th` resync: stop AVR + `foldBackToLocal()`; high uncertainty: keep `1 Hz` maintenance pings active and flag degraded sync in stats/HUD. |

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative A: Importing `:sendspin` from `:fcast`

*   **Pros:** Groups Sendspin-named code inside `:sendspin`.
*   **Cons:** Creates a circular Gradle dependency (`:sendspin` already depends on `:fcast`), and `:sendspin` wraps pre-1.0 `sendspin-jvm:0.2.3` (unrelated to Spec 0's control protocol).
*   **Reason for Rejection:** Extracting `AudioCodecTokens` + `SendspinAvr*` into `:fcast` keeps the Gradle graph acyclic and leaves `:sendspin` untouched until its future 1.0 Noise upgrade.

### Alternative B: Per-Speaker ARCore Anchors or Expanding `:xrplayer`'s 15-Key Preference Allowlist

*   **Pros:** Tracks speakers independently in ARCore and stores room state in player-local `SharedPreferences`.
*   **Cons:** 13–16 per-speaker anchors partially fail in dim rooms, `ActivitySpace` resets across kills, overloading `pref_xr_player_wall_anchor_uuid` breaks standalone wall-pinning and multi-room setups, and new `:xrplayer` keys violate `ArchitectureGuardsTest`.
*   **Reason for Rejection:** Storing per-`server_id` `{ anchorUuid, anchorToMlpTransform }` in main-process `RememberedReceiversStore` + canonical room profile on `spatialfin-avr` delivers atomic multi-room restore with zero allowlist expansion.

### Alternative C: Dual-Mode `SplitAvController` (With ExoPlayer Speed-Trim & `ProtocolAdapter`) vs. `SplitAvSessionEngine`

*   **Pros:** Leaves transport-level branching inside existing `SplitAvController` or wires `ProtocolAdapter` into `CastAdapterFactory`.
*   **Cons:** Speed-trim causes XR cadence judder and fights `SurfaceFrameRateController`; forcing opposite clock-leadership loops through a 15-method interface bloats `SplitAvController` and risks FCast regressions; `ProtocolAdapter` models full A+V casting.
*   **Reason for Rejection:** A narrow `SplitAvSessionEngine` interface separates `FCastSplitAvEngine` and `SendspinAvrSplitAvEngine` with lock-free `< 250 ms` App Lock cancellation.

### Alternative D: Android-Side Calibration DSP Solving or USB Raw UAC2 vs. Pure-HTTP `AudioRecord` Capture

*   **Pros:** Avoids transferring raw sweep WAVs over LAN HTTP or bypasses Android `AudioRecord` HAL resampling.
*   **Cons:** Running deconvolution, acoustic phase alignment, and Cavern/REW biquad synthesis on Android duplicates Spec 2's Rust DSP solver and increases APK complexity; `UsbManager` raw isochronous UAC2 breaks across vendor hubs and pops permission dialogs.
*   **Reason for Rejection:** `AudioManager.getDevices()` + streaming 24-bit WAV over pure HTTP multipart into `spatialfin-avr` (`Spec 2`) keeps DSP solving single-sourced and hardware capture uniform across Beam devices.

### Alternative E: Reusing `ContentKeyManager` Download DEK & Hosting `XrRoomCalibrationOverlay` in `:xrplayer`

*   **Pros:** Reuses existing download encryption and `:player:xr` overlay navigation.
*   **Cons:** Coupling room tokens to `ContentKeyManager` alters `:xrplayer` DEK behavior when App Lock is Off and purges paired rooms whenever App Lock resets; hosting calibration in `:xrplayer` lacks Bearer tokens and fails `SplitAvBridgeService`'s active-playback registration check.
*   **Reason for Rejection:** A dedicated non-auth-bound `AndroidKeyStore` AES-256-GCM alias in `TheaterAvrTokenStore` and hosting `XrRoomCalibrationOverlay` in main-process `UnifiedMainActivity` eliminate both failure modes.

## Risks & Limits

| Risk / Failure Mode | Category | Impact / Limit | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Cleartext trusted-LAN transport (`ws://`/`http://`)** | Security | `avr_token` and `jellyfin_token` traverse local Wi-Fi unless fronted by an operator TLS reverse proxy. | Document trusted-LAN scope; support `useTls` (`wss://`/`https://`), disable HTTP redirects, enforce Spec 0 §3 URL/token checks, and clear in-memory token references on stop/App Lock. |
| **Wi-Fi RTT asymmetry under 4K UHD bursts** | Timing / Sync | Downlink 4K bursts (`60–120 Mbps`) spike RTT and starve single-minimum filters. | Use bounded sliding-window min-RTT (`16` / `30 s`), report `clock_uncertainty_us` on `2 Hz` beacons, and keep `1 Hz` maintenance pings active when `>= 2_000 µs`. |
| **Beam USB-C glasses vs `UMIK-1` port contention** | Hardware UX | Beam phones have one USB-C port, preventing simultaneous DisplayPort glasses and `UMIK-1` unless via a hub. | Document glasses-unplugged or powered USB-C PD hub with DisplayPort Alt Mode (`DP-Alt`), or Spec 2's laptop `avr-capture-cli`. |
| **Tier 2 WAV footprint, USB disconnect & Android HAL quirks** | Calibration / Resource | 12-channel sweeps reach `~35 MiB` (capped at `64 MiB` on upload); `24BIT_PACKED` fails on some HALs; OEM `MIC` DSP corrupts phase; USB unplug or Doze aborts sweeps. | Check `>= 64 MiB` cache space, delete temps in `finally`, negotiate `PCM_FLOAT -> 24-bit WAV`, warn if `UNPROCESSED` missing, hold wake-lock, and abort via `DELETE /sweeps/{sweep_id}` on negative `read()` / `AudioDeviceCallback`. |
| **Segmented VLANs & session/App Lock resets** | Discovery / Lifecycle | Multi-VLAN routers block mDNS; `session_busy` or `401` interrupts playback; biometric resets affect key state. | Support manual `host:port` + TLS and QR URI pairing; prompt explicitly on `force_takeover`; handle all Spec 0 error/push codes; keep `TheaterAvrTokenStore` decoupled from download DEK resets. |
| **Local HLS transcode fallback PTS reset** | Playback Sync | Local fallback transcodes reset `presentationTimeUs` to `0`, risking multi-minute beacon skew. | Unify remote selection via `SplitAvLaunchPlan` upfront and add active `startTimeTicks` offset inside `PlayerSplitAvAdapter` (`SplitAvStreamUrlPolicy`). |
| **Galaxy XR `0 lux` anchor & hand-tracking loss** | XR Spatial | Optical SLAM relocalization (`<= 2_500 ms`) and gaze-pinch hand tracking degrade in `0 lux`. | Spawn flat screen at frame 0 from gravity + `lcr_screen_plane`; render non-blocking 2-button `Play Center Chime` + `Lock Here` banner accepting controller or gaze input. |
| **`:xrplayer` `Process.killProcess()` teardown race** | Process IPC | Killing `:xrplayer` before `onDestroy()` could leave loud room audio playing. | Normal Back exits send synchronous `MSG_UNREGISTER(USER_EXIT)` (`< 250 ms` stop); unexpected kills fire `IBinder.DeathRecipient` -> immediate `playback.pause(flush=true)` + `1.5 s` grace (`6_000 ms` Spec 0 TTL backstop). |

## Non-Functional Requirements

*   **Security & Privacy:** Per-AVR Bearer tokens and `client_id` are encrypted in the main process via a dedicated non-auth-bound `AndroidKeyStore` AES-256-GCM alias (`TheaterAvrTokenStore`) and deleted on unpair or `AppDataWiper` (`PreWipeHook`); `stream_url` rejects/strips URL userinfo and case-insensitive `api_key=`/`token=` (`?` or `&`); `jellyfin_token` rejects `\r`, `\n`, `\0` and logs are scrubbed unconditionally; App Lock and user switch clear token references and stop AVR sessions in `< 250 ms`.
*   **Performance & Latency:** ExoPlayer video speed locked at `1.0000f` on `StandaloneMediaClock`; lock-free `@Volatile` `VideoFrameMetadataListener` µs capture; `2 Hz` 4-field beacons; streaming 24-bit WAV file export (zero full-file RAM buffer); `SplitAvBridgeService` dispatched on a dedicated `HandlerThread`.
*   **Reliability & Resource Cleanup:** `5 s` `playback.ready` deadline and `> 3` retry cap fall back to local playback; `SplitAvBridgeService` `IBinder.DeathRecipient` issues a pause and tears down in `1.5 s` (`6_000 ms` server `heartbeat_ttl_ms` backstop); `Umik1CaptureEngine` frees temp WAV files in `finally` on success, abort, or USB disconnect; `RememberedReceiversStore` exempts paired rooms from pruning.
*   **Rollout & Rollback:** `FCastSplitAvEngine` preserves legacy FCast Split A/V behavior and existing `SplitAvReplayTest` fixtures unmodified behind `SplitAvSessionEngine`; `RememberedReceiversStore` additions use nullable defaults (`ignoreUnknownKeys = true`); Phases 1–3 build and ship independently.
