Smart Playback, Offline Downloads & Security

SpatialFin matches audio and subtitle tracks to your language rules, renders styled ASS/SSA typesetting natively, skips intros offline or online, transcodes troublesome files locally on-device, and optionally locks offline media behind AES-256-CTR encryption.

What It Does & Optionality

Every playback enhancement works independently across Jellyfin servers, SMB/NFS shares, and local storage: use Smart Language selection without downloading anything, keep unencrypted or AES-256-CTR encrypted downloads under Download/SpatialFin, or enable Biometric/PIN App Lock when sharing a device or headset.

Smart Playback, Sync & Subtitles

Smart Language & Forced Subtitle Rules

  • Configure an ordered list of preferred spoken audio languages plus an optional Prefer original language audio toggle and per-series track overrides.
  • Forced & Signs vs. Full Dialogue: When the selected audio matches your spoken language, SpatialFin automatically picks matching forced/signs subtitles (controlled by Smart Forced Subtitles) without subtitling English dialogue; when audio is foreign, it picks full dialogue subtitles in your preferred language.
  • All pre-playback track chips and in-player pickers resolve through the same Jellyfin MediaStreams + container language matcher so the track shown before playback matches what starts playing.

Native libass, Live A/V Sync & Stats for Nerds

  • Native libass_jni Renderer: Complex karaoke typography, signs, vector graphics, and multi-layer ASS/SSA positioning render directly onto transparent RGBA overlays with bundled Noto Sans + system/embedded fallback fonts (including CJK coverage).
  • Live Timing Offsets: Adjust subtitle timing live within ±10 s and audio delay within ±5 s (via a PCM delay ring buffer for positive delay or sink clock shift for negative delay).
  • Diagnostics HUD: Toggle Stats for Nerds inside player controls to inspect codec, HDR/Dolby Vision metadata, decoder name, refresh-rate matching, dropped frames, and buffer health.

4-Tier Intro & Outro Skip

Skip detection resolves segments automatically in priority order:

  1. Jellyfin Media Segments API (10.10+ native segments).
  2. Legacy Intro Skipper plugin endpoint (/Episode/{id}/IntroTimestamps).
  3. Embedded MKV/MP4 Chapter Titles: Client regex detection (ChapterSegmentDetector) matches OP/ED/Intro/Outro chapters even offline or on SMB/NFS shares.
  4. AniSkip Crowd Timestamps: Optional anime OP/ED fallback via MAL ID when enabled in Settings.

Offline Downloads, Media3 Transcoding & SMB/NFS

  • Resumable Offline Vault (Download/SpatialFin): Downloaded files are stored under Download/SpatialFin with download integrity checks and an optional Delete after watching toggle.
  • On-Device Media3 Offline Transcoding: If a downloaded or local file triggers a codec/container decode error during airplane mode (or via pre-flight download optimization), SpatialFin invokes Media3 Transformer locally to convert the video into universal H.264/AAC MP4 with live progress and cancellation.
  • Local Folders, Open With & SMB/NFS: Play local device files directly via Android Open with intents or mount SMB/NFS shares with optional TMDB metadata matching.

App Lock, AES-256-CTR Downloads & Threat Model

  • App Lock Modes: Lock access to SpatialFin (including switching away from protected screens) using device Biometrics or a local PBKDF2 PIN.
  • Encrypted Offline Media: Enable encrypted downloads to store cached video files as AES-256-CTR ciphertexts decrypted streamingly during local playback inside SpatialFin rather than left readable in clear MP4/MKV files.
  • Local Threat Model & Recovery Boundary: Encryption keys and App Lock credentials stay strictly on-device inside Android keystore/encrypted preferences—neither Jellyfin nor Companion Hub holds copies. This protects casual shared-device access and filesystem snooping on lost devices, while root-level memory inspection while unlocked remains outside an app-level threat model.

Verify It Works

  • Play an anime or multi-track MKV item with stylized ASS signs and confirm crisp typesetting renders without burning in on the server.
  • Download a test episode, enable Airplane Mode, and start playback from the Downloads tab.

Troubleshooting

  • Subtitle timing drifts by a fixed offset: Open in-player Subtitle Sync and shift by -10 s to +10 s per track.
  • Local/downloaded file fails to decode offline: Accept the in-player Transcode for Offline Playback recovery prompt (Media3 Transformer) to produce an H.264/AAC copy locally.
  • SMB/NFS movie shows filename only: Enable TMDB metadata lookup in the network share source configuration.
