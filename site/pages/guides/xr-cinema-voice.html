Android XR Cinema & Voice AI Guide

SpatialFin takes full advantage of Jetpack SceneCore on Android XR headsets—separating Home Space multitasking from curved Full Space cinema, ARCore wall pinning, 3D spatial audio, and local-first Voice AI.

What It Does & Optionality

You can browse your library as a movable window in Home Space or launch playback into a dedicated Full Space theater—with optional passthrough dimming, cylindrical screen curvature, ARCore wall pinning, and voice queries backed by your active subtitle buffer. Voice models are completely optional; you can browse and watch without downloading any AI model.

Prerequisites

  • Android XR headset running SpatialFin v{{version}} (API {{minSdk}}+, libre track).
  • For local on-device voice inference: either system Gemini Nano (AICore) or the local LiteRT-LM model (gemma-4-E2B-it.litertlm) downloaded in Settings, or an optional Gemini API key for cloud fallback (gemini-3.1-flash-lite-preview).
  • For Android phone voice assistant: supported Android phone with microphone permission granted to SpatialFin.

Step-by-Step Configuration

Home Space vs. Full Space & Panel Persistence

  • Browsing Panel Scale: Resize the SpatialFin Home Space browsing panel to comfort; SpatialFin persists the browsing panel scale (xrAppPanelScale) directly via SceneCore SpatialPanel.
  • Free-Floating Player Placement: Launching playback enters Full Space and spawns the floating cinema screen centered in front of you while preserving your preferred screen size (xrPlayerPanelScale) and depth distance (xrPlayerPanelDepth).
  • Multitask Mode: On an item detail page, tap Multitask to watch video inside Home Space alongside other headset windows.

Curved Cinema Presets, Passthrough & Wall Anchoring

  • Screen Curvature: Inside player spatial settings, choose between Flat, Subtle (9.0 m radius), Cinema (6.5 m radius), and Deep (5.2 m radius). Curvature uses a 48-segment cylindrical custom mesh and automatically falls back to flat quad geometry for wall-pinned, VR180°, or 360° streams.
  • Graduated Passthrough Dimming: Adjust passthrough opacity smoothly from 0% (darkened theater void) to 100% (full room view).
  • ARCore Wall Anchoring: For flat 2D cinema playback, choose Pin to Wall to lock the screen onto a detected vertical ARCore wall plane. When cold-starting later playback with a saved wall anchor inside your field of view, SpatialFin restores that exact room placement automatically.

3D Formats & Spatial Audio

  • SpatialFin auto-detects filenames for 3D Side-by-Side (SBS), Top-Bottom (TAB), VR180, and 360° equirectangular projection, or lets you override stereo/projection manually in player settings.
  • For flat-screen PCM audio streams, 3D Positional Audio attaches a SceneCore SpatialAudioTrack point source directly to the virtual screen entity (automatically bypassed for passthrough, 180°/360°, and Split-A/V casting).

XR Hand-Gesture & Phone Voice AI

  1. Open Voice Control: Hold an open palm facing the headset in XR (or tap the floating mic button on an Android phone) to start listening.
  2. Ask Contextual Questions: Ask about dialogue, plot recaps, or cast members—SpatialFin includes the last 20 minutes of spoken subtitle lines (backed by a rolling memory + disk cache buffer) and reads aloud answers while auto-resuming video after speech finishes.
  3. Identify Actors ("Who is this?"): Matching a character or cast question against current item people metadata displays an interactive Spatial Cast Card showing credits and other titles starring that actor in your library.
  4. Hands-Free Interrupt: Form a closed fist in XR (or tap the mic overlay) at any time to stop assistant speech immediately.

Verify It Works

  • Changing curvature from Flat to Cinema (6.5 m) curves the virtual theater screen around your field of view while subtitles remain centered and legible.
  • Adjusting the passthrough slider smoothly dims or reveals your physical room behind the screen.
  • Speaking "What did they just say?" returns context grounded in recent subtitle cues without drifting into unrelated media.

Troubleshooting

  • Curved preset button shows flat screen: Curved cylindrical meshes are intentionally suppressed while Wall Pin, VR180, or 360° projection is active; unpin or switch back to flat 2D playback to apply curvature.
  • No 3D positional audio effect: Switch audio output from bitstream passthrough to PCM decoding, and ensure Split-A/V remote audio is off, since SpatialAudioTrack requires local PCM audio.
  • Local voice model slow to respond on first turn: On-device LiteRT-LM inference initializes on demand when voice is first invoked; playback pauses cleanly during GPU inference and resumes once streaming finishes.
