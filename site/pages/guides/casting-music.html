Casting, Split-A/V & Music Assistant Guide

Cast video to TVs and headsets over FCast, Google Cast, or AirPlay v1 video, route private Split-A/V earbud audio to a phone or watch, and drive whole-home queues with Music Assistant and SendSpin.

What It Does & Optionality

Every casting and music engine is optional and runs on-demand when you select a target receiver. You can switch targets mid-session between full-screen casting, Split-A/V private audio, LAN remote control across two SpatialFin instances, SyncPlay watch parties, or synced Music Assistant rooms.

Prerequisites

  • SpatialFin v{{version}} on the sending and/or receiving device.
  • Devices reachable on the same LAN/subnet (or over WebRTC for supported remote Music Assistant features).
  • For Wear OS Split-A/V private audio: paired SpatialFin for Wear OS watch with Bluetooth earbuds connected to the watch.

Step-by-Step Usage

Multi-Protocol Video Casting (FCast, Google Cast & AirPlay v1)

  1. Tap the Cast button from an item sheet or active player to open the receiver picker (discovery runs simultaneously across protocols).
  2. FCast v4 (Native Receiver & Sender): Stream high-bitrate media between SpatialFin XR, TV, phones, and WebXR while preserving subtitle fidelity (including automatic burn-in fallback when a receiver cannot draw ASS/SSA natively).
  3. Google Cast: Cast directly to Chromecast and Android TV / Google TV targets on your LAN.
  4. AirPlay v1 (Video Only): Discover and cast to unencrypted AirPlay v1 video targets. Limitation: audio-only AirPlay speakers and encrypted AirPlay 2 pairing are not supported.

Split-A/V Private Earbud Audio

Keep HDR or 3D video on the headset or TV screen while routing private audio to personal headphones paired with your phone, Wear OS watch, or browser:

  1. Start playback on Android XR or TV, then select a Split-A/V audio target from the Cast or Wear OS private-audio toggle.
  2. Open live Audio Sync controls (±5 s offset) to align Bluetooth/network latency precisely to lip movement.

Theater Audio (spatialfin-avr Multi-Room & Multi-Channel Split-A/V)

Pair Galaxy XR, Android Phone/Beam, Android TV, or WebXR (/web/) with an optional self-hosted spatialfin-avr Docker daemon to render HDR/3D video on your display while playing synchronized 24-bit/48 kHz stereo, surround, or Atmos audio through physical speakers across one or more rooms:

  • Multi-Room Server, Persistence & Embedded Web UI: Host multiple independent rooms (e.g., bedroom 2.0 SendSpin speakers, living room HDMI soundbar, family room 7.1 ESP32 array) on one spatialfin-avr server. Room volume, mute, EQ bypass, upmix mode, and crossover edits persist automatically across restarts and sync across every paired client. Manage everything from any browser at http://<avr-host>:<port>/ with live playback controls, an interactive 2D SVG speaker floorplan, inline SVG QR pairing, paired-client revocation, and write-only Music Assistant configuration.
  • Mixed Audio Outputs per Room: Combine LAN _sendspin._tcp WebSocket speakers (with mDNS scan and identification chimes), direct host PC HDMI/ALSA playback ports (2.0–7.1 with startup-cached format/channel diagnostics), and encrypted Noise KKpsk2 TCP (:8937) sinks—including the unified ESP32-C5/S3 ESP-IDF firmware supporting 2-ch stereo I²S and 8-ch TDM over compact lossless FLAC framing—with cross-room conflict protection.
  • Discovery, Pairing & Opt-In Dual-Mode TLS: Auto-discovers _spatialfin-avr._tcp.local. on your LAN (or add manually by host:port or scan a pairing QR code). Pairing uses a single-use 120 s 6-digit PIN and shares one Android Keystore token across all rooms on the server. When built-in TLS is enabled on the AVR, Android devices automatically pin the certificate SPKI from the pairing QR (or confirm an 8-character SAS code on Android TV) with sticky anti-downgrade protection.
  • In-Player Multi-Room Switcher, TrueHD Atmos OAMD & TV Remote Controls: Switch active playback cleanly between paired rooms or local device audio in two taps without restarting the player. Video stays locked at 1.0000× while the AVR decodes TrueHD Atmos Object Audio Metadata (oamd) via 3D VBAP with live HUD object-count / bed-fallback telemetry, exposes live master volume, delay-invariant EQ Bypass, and all 4 upmix modes (Native, All-Channel Stereo, DTS Height Upmix, Neural Height), and intercepts Android TV remote Volume +/−/Mute keys with HDMI refresh-rate settling.
  • Acoustic Lip-Sync Auto-Calibration, CamillaDSP, UMIK-1 & XR 6DoF Lock: Run Auto-Calibrate Lip-Sync from your phone or headset microphone to measure room audio hardware delay (fixed_hw_delay_us) via timed chirp bursts, run per-channel chimes/pink noise, upload REW/CamillaDSP Room EQ bundles, capture tripod-mounted USB-C OTG UMIK-1 sweeps, or pin a front-wall anchor on Galaxy XR for LCR Screen Lock with a dark-room Play Center Chime / Lock Here fallback.
  • Music Assistant Handoff: Configure Music Assistant in the AVR Web UI or via SPATIALFIN_AVR_MA_URL so active Music Assistant playback pauses cleanly before cinema playback claims a speaker's SendSpin slot (with optional auto-resume on stop).

SyncPlay & LAN Remote Control

  • SyncPlay Groups: Create or join Jellyfin SyncPlay rooms directly from playback controls to keep play/pause and seek synchronized across remote viewers.
  • SpatialFin LAN Remote: Control another running SpatialFin instance from your phone or tablet—with an interactive scrub timeline, chapter selector with thumbnails, -10 s / +10 s jumps, and a continuous volume slider.

Music Assistant & SendSpin Synchronized Audio

  • Open the Music tab and point SpatialFin at your local Music Assistant server.
  • Manage player queues, browse playlists, trigger party shuffle/radio modes, and turn SpatialFin into a live SendSpin synchronized multi-room audio endpoint alongside other speakers.

Off-LAN Music Assistant WebRTC Scope

When connected off-LAN via WebRTC, SendSpin audio playback, home rows, cover art, and basic play/pause/volume/group controls work. Full library search and deep item-detail browsing currently require a LAN/VPN connection, and TURN relays for symmetric hard-NAT networks are still pending.

Verify It Works

  • Open the Cast sheet and verify nearby FCast, Google Cast, or SpatialFin LAN targets appear with protocol labels.
  • Toggle Private audio on Wear OS or pair an FCast audio receiver and verify headset speakers mute cleanly while watch/phone audio stays synced.

Troubleshooting

  • Audio lags slightly behind video during Split-A/V: Use the live audio delay slider (±5 s range) to shift audio timing until dialog matches lip motion.
  • Audio-only AirPlay HomePod/speaker missing or fails pairing: SpatialFin implements unencrypted AirPlay v1 video casting only; use Google Cast, FCast, or SendSpin for audio-only multi-room targets.
  • No FCast targets visible from WebXR: Browser WebSocket security requires wss:// via Tailscale Serve or reverse proxy—see the WebXR Guide.
