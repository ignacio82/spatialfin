Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a Jellyfin server to use SpatialFin?

No. SpatialFin connects to Jellyfin 10.10+ servers, or runs standalone against local device folders, SMB/NFS network shares, and QuickJS source plugins.

Is SpatialFin Companion Hub required? Is it the same as the Wear OS app?

No to both. SpatialFin Companion Hub is an optional self-hosted Docker service for QR Quick Connect brokering, SearXNG web search, and remote logs. SpatialFin for Wear OS is the separate smartwatch app installed from Google Play for wrist controls and Split-A/V earbud audio.

Do I have to search separately for XR, Phone, TV, and Wear OS on Google Play?

No. All three native tracks (libre, tv, and wear) ship under the single dev.spatialfin Google Play listing so Play delivers the matching build per device automatically.

Does SpatialFin collect telemetry or personal analytics?

Neither the native Android apps nor the WebXR app collect analytics or crash telemetry. Only these static documentation pages at spatialfin.martinez.fyi record cookieless page-view counts via self-hosted Umami (loaded on docs routes, omitted from redirect stubs). See the Privacy Policy for details.
