SpatialFin: The Ultimate XR-First Jellyfin Client

SpatialFin is not just another media player - it is a premium Jellyfin client built for the way you actually watch. Whether diving into a private virtual cinema on your Galaxy XR headset, catching a series on your phone, or relaxing with Android TV, SpatialFin delivers a fluid, high-performance experience tailored to every screen.

Immersive XR & Spatial Video
Designed from the ground up for Android XR and spatial computing:
- Flexible Spaces: Immerse yourself in a virtual cinema in Full Space, or multitask with a floating, resizable window in Home Space alongside other apps.
- Spatial & 3D Video: Hardware-accelerated playback for Side-by-Side (SBS), Over-Under (OU), and Apple Vision MV-HEVC spatial video files.
- Persistent Layouts: Position and scale your screen anywhere in your physical environment; SpatialFin remembers exactly where you left it.

Context-Aware Voice Assistant
Ditch the cumbersome on-screen keyboard with an intelligent, media-aware voice assistant:
- Conversational Control: Ask naturally: "Play the next episode," "Find a sci-fi comedy under 90 minutes," or "What did they just say?" to immediately rewind with dialogue captions.
- Privacy-First Intelligence: Fast on-device AI processing powered by local models, with optional cloud fallback.

Tailored for Every Android Screen
- Android TV & Google TV: Full D-pad Leanback interface with Google TV "Watch Next" launcher integration and system-wide voice search.
- Mobile & Handhelds: Intuitive touch navigation with gesture scrubbing, picture-in-picture, and a dedicated mic action button for voice commands on the go.

Pixel-Perfect Subtitles & Audio
- libass Native Engine: Razor-sharp subtitle rendering designed specifically to eliminate blur and aliasing on high-angular-resolution XR displays.
- Full Styling Support: Flawless positioning and typography for ASS/SSA anime subtitles, styled fonts, signs, dialogue effects, and karaoke text.
- Smart Language Logic: Automatic audio and subtitle track matching based on your language preferences, with per-series remember rules.

Wear OS Companion
Turn your smartwatch into the ultimate media controller:
- Rotary Crown Scrubbing: Precision timeline seek with haptic detent stepping, volume control, and rapid transport actions right from your wrist.
- Wear OS Tiles: Instantly jump back into your media with the "Up Next" carousel tile, or manage current playback via the "Now Playing" tile.
- Wear OS Complications: Add complications to your favorite watch faces to monitor playback status and headset battery levels at a glance.
- Private Audio Streaming: Route cinema audio directly to watch-paired Bluetooth earbuds while video stays on your headset or TV screen.
- Quick Switchers: Change audio tracks, toggle subtitles, jump chapters, or calibrate spatial viewing angles without opening a menu on screen.

Built for Power Users
- Offline Downloads: Download movies and episodes directly to device storage for travel and flights.
- Direct Network Shares: Stream directly from local SMB and NFS network shares with zero server transcoding overhead.
- SyncPlay: Watch in perfect synchronization with friends and family across rooms or continents.
- Multi-Server & Quick Pair: Seamlessly switch between multiple Jellyfin servers and pair new devices in seconds using companion QR codes.

Note: A personal Jellyfin server is required for media streaming. SpatialFin is an independent third-party client and is not affiliated with the official Jellyfin project.
